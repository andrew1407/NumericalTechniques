Numerical Techniques Labs

The NumericalTechniques labs, running in your browser. Each C++ program is compiled unchanged to WebAssembly with Emscripten; the page runs its main() and charts what it prints.

Every result comes from the lab's own compiled program. Curves marked JS are reference values computed by this page for comparison, not by the lab. On charts, drag to zoom, double-click to reset, and click a legend entry to hide a series.
